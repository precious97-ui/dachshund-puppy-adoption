Our Story

Raised with Love,
Not Just for Sale

At Little Sausage Haven, every Dachshund is treated like family from the moment they arrive. We focus on health, temperament, and early socialization so your new puppy is ready to thrive in a loving home.

Our puppies are raised in a home environment, exposed to everyday sounds, children, and gentle handling. We provide full health records, starter care kits, and lifetime support for every family who adopts from us.
